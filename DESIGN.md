## Brand & Style

This design system expresses an intimate, literary sanctuary: a digital love letter traversing continents and oceans, bridging the sun-drenched sands and Nile twilight of Egypt with the lush emerald jade and Caribbean warmth of Honduras. It evokes the feeling of unfolding handmade parchment under soft, flickering candlelight while stars stretch across an endless midnight sky.

The aesthetic marries classical editorial romance with contemporary digital craftsmanship. Drawing from warm romanticism, candlelit tonal layering, and quiet luxury, the UI behaves like an illuminated keepsake. Surfaces glow subtly from within rather than projecting stark, artificial light. Interactions are lingering, graceful, and serene, avoiding jarring immediacy in favor of delicate fades, gentle scale shifts, and whisper-soft transitions.

The emotional atmosphere is tender, revered, and deeply devoted. Visual elements reflect tactile artifacts: deckle-edged keepsakes, wax seals, warm metallic leafing, and translucent vellum overlays. The experience invites unhurried contemplation, honoring distance collapsed through devotion.

## Colors

The color palette captures the geography and intimacy of two worlds converging in devotion:

- **Primary (`#8B263E` — Terciopelo Borgoña):** A rich, velvety wine tone embodying emotional depth, passion, and the tactile richness of aged vintage ink and rose petals. Used for focal callouts, active indicators, romantic highlights, and primary points of engagement.
- **Secondary (`#D4A373` — Oro Champán & Arena del Nilo):** A warm, radiant champagne gold evoking Egyptian desert twilight, candle flame warmth, and antique gilded manuscripts. Serves as the primary romantic accent, subtle borders, luminous text headers, and delicate ornamentation.
- **Tertiary (`#2D5A4C` — Jade Caribeño & Esmeralda Copán):** A deep, nocturnal emerald representing the vibrant landscapes, tropical flora, and lush mountainous mist of Honduras. Deployed sparingly for grounding moments, natural motifs, and subtle complementary balance.
- **Neutral Palette (`#1A1416` base):** Deep midnight espresso tinted with warm plum and carbon ash. Rather than cold synthetic blacks, background surfaces use `#120E10` (void), `#1A1416` (base canvas), `#251C20` (vellum surface), and `#34272C` (elevated keepsake card).
- **Text & Accents:** High-tier copy glows in `#F5EBE6` (warm alabaster) and `#D8C3BE` (soft rose parchment), maintaining effortless legibility while preserving the candlelit mood. Critical interactive states and glowing embers employ a delicate coral blush (`#E07A5F`).

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances classical literary grace with effortless modern readability. 

- **Display & Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Carries the emotional weight of handwritten missives, literary dedication, and poetic verse. High stroke contrast and delicate serifs bring a classical editorial presence. Italic cuts are reserved for intimate thoughts, epigraphs, and dedications in Spanish.
- **Body & Functional UI (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Provides a warm, contemporary counterpoint. Its softly curved letterforms maintain supreme legibility across darker backgrounds without geometric coldness. It honors long-form prose and poetic narrative with generous line-heights to allow the words room to breathe.
- **Text Composition:** All poetic passages, dates, and geographic markers use slightly relaxed line-heights and intentional letter-spacing for labels to invoke luxury stationery and archival engraving.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy is centered around a fluid editorial grid that prioritizes narrative rhythm over dense utility. Content breathes within generous margins, simulating wide manuscript mats and quiet gallery spaces.

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop views employ an 8-column or 12-column reading grid with a max-width container of `1180px`, keeping intimate text passages within comfortable reading lengths (`680px` centered for continuous epistolary prose). Mobile views collapse cleanly into a single fluid column flanked by safe margins.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Sections and narrative verses separate via expansive rhythmic intervals (`space-xl` scaled with dynamic viewport percentages). White space (or dark candlelit space) functions as an intentional pause between stanzas.
- **Reflow Behavior:** On touchscreens, margins tighten while inner component padding remains commodious to ensure touch target comfort and retain an airy, uncrowded parchment feel.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this design system is rendered not through sharp drop shadows, but through warm candlelight illumination, tonal diffusion, and ethereal translucency.

- **Tonal Layers:** The background sits in deep obsidian-wine (`#120E10`). Content layers rise upward through translucent surfaces (`#1E1619` with 70–85% alpha) paired with subtle background blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), evoking fine vellum resting atop mahogany.
- **Ambient Candlelit Glows:** Elements project gentle, wide-spread radial glows tinted with warm secondary gold (`rgba(212, 163, 115, 0.08)`) or primary velvet burgundy (`rgba(139, 38, 62, 0.15)`). Hover states increase luminescence rather than casting harsh drop shadows.
- **Ghost Filigree Outlines:** Floating parchment cards, dialogs, and keepsakes utilize fine 1px borders rendered in gold or rose-gold at low opacities (`rgba(212, 163, 115, 0.2)` to `rgba(212, 163, 115, 0.4)`), mimicking gilded book edges catching soft firelight.

## Shapes

The design uses roundedness level `2` (0.5rem / 8px base radius, scaling to 1rem / 16px for cards and 1.5rem / 24px for large dialogs and visual frames).

This level of curvature strikes a harmonious balance: soft enough to avoid the severity of sharp technical boxes, yet structured enough to preserve the dignity and poise of classic hand-bound correspondence. Buttons, photo frames of Tegucigalpa or Cairo, interactive audio players, and floating note seals gently round at their corners, feeling organic, approachable, and timeless.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Sellar Promesa"):** Velvet burgundy (`#8B263E`) background with gilded champagne text (`#F5EBE6`), enclosed in a subtle champagne border (`rgba(212, 163, 115, 0.3)`). Hover initiates a warm amber radial bloom and a micro-scale shift (`scale(1.02)`).
- **Secondary ("Leer Poema"):** Translucent vellum surface (`rgba(212, 163, 115, 0.08)`) with a delicate 1px border (`rgba(212, 163, 115, 0.35)`), glowing softly on focus or hover.
- **Text Link:** Warm gold text accompanied by an animated underline that draws outward from the center upon hover.

### Romantic Keepsake Cards
- Built with deep translucent backgrounds (`rgba(37, 28, 32, 0.75)`), blurred backdrops, and an ultra-fine 1px border glowing in faint champagne gold. Cards display poetic excerpts, shared memories, or songs. Inside, a subtle top-right watermark or foil insignia can represent the coordinates of Egypt and Honduras.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact pill-shaped metadata tags (e.g., *"El Nilo"*, *"Caribe"*, *"Siempre"*) rendered in deep jade (`#2D5A4C`) or muted terracotta blush (`rgba(224, 122, 95, 0.15)`) with high-contrast, fine-lettered labels.

### Input Fields & Letter Scribes
- Styled like quiet journal entries. Transparent backgrounds flanked by a single bottom border or subtle full rounded border in muted sand (`rgba(212, 163, 115, 0.25)`). Focus states illuminate the border in bright champagne gold with an ambient candlelight aura. Placeholder text appears in soft italic parchment tone.

### Lists & Timeline Milestones
- Connected by a delicate vertical thread in antique gold. Milestones feature glowing gem-like nodes (alternating emerald jade and wine burgundy) signifying key memories spanning distances and dates.

### Audio / Ambient Sound Player
- A dedicated minimalist component for playing Nile night-breezes or soft guitar melodies. Rendered with custom slim waveform bars that pulse between burgundy and champagne hues.
